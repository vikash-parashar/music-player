Ampli Kavach privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Ampli Kavach is a scam-call and scam-message shield for Android, made by Ampli Studio ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Android app (package com.vikash.ampli.kavach) and the optional Kavach server behind its online features.

Ampli Kavach is not a government app. It is not linked to any government body, police, bank or telecom operator. When it helps you file a complaint, it opens the official website (for example cybercrime.gov.in or Chakshu on sancharsaathi.gov.in), and you submit the complaint yourself.

Contact for privacy questions, complaints or deletion requests: support@ampliapps.in

1. What stays on your phone

Kavach does its work on your phone. We never upload these:

  • Your contacts. Kavach reads them only to show names and to let your contacts' calls through. It never uploads your contact list, unlike some caller-ID apps.
  • Your call log. If you make Kavach your Phone app, it reads and writes the call log on the phone to show your calls, block spam and show call stats.
  • Your SMS messages. If you make Kavach your SMS app, your messages are stored, sorted (Important, OTP, Bank, Offers, Spam) and checked for scam links on the phone. Message text is never sent to us.
  • Block lists, notes, labels, reminders, settings and saved evidence (screenshots and recordings you choose to save for a complaint).
  • Backups you export go to a file you choose. There is no Kavach cloud backup.

2. Online protection (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Settings › Online protection, the app uses the Kavach server:

What is sent When Why
A random install ID and a signed token Once, when you turn it on So the server can rate-limit and let you delete your data. It is not your name, phone number or a hardware ID.
One phone number you look up, or that calls you and is not in your contacts On that call or lookup To show a name and spam score
A number you report as spam or "not spam", with a category and an optional short note When you report To warn other users
A name you suggest for a number, or the network caller name (CNAP) shown for an incoming non-contact call When you suggest one, or on that call To show names for unknown numbers

Only that one number is sent. The server stores numbers only as keyed hashes, not as plain numbers. Lookups are not stored. The app also downloads a daily list of reported spam numbers and checks it on the phone. A name suggested by only one person is never shown to anyone.

Delete my data: Settings › Delete my data from the server removes everything your install sent (reports, names, labels) at once. You can also email us.

3. Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ampli Kavach Pro, or the "24 hours ad-free" reward, removes them. Ads never appear on call screens, the caller card or the complaint and emergency screens.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostics, and share them with Google and its partners. In the EEA and the UK, Kavach asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases

Pro (₹149, one time) and tips are bought through Google Play. We receive only the purchase result from Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details.

5. Permissions

Kavach asks for each permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it:

  • Call screening / Phone app role, call log, contacts, phone state, make calls: to identify, block and place calls.
  • SMS app role and SMS permissions: to receive, show, send and filter messages, on the phone only.
  • Display over other apps: for the caller card shown on top of another Phone app.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications: for incoming calls, scam warnings and reminders.
  • Camera: to scan a QR code before you pay.
  • Query installed apps: to warn you about remote-access and fake loan apps (Phone Check).
  • Advertising ID: for ads (section 3).

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data leaves your phone only as described in sections 2 and 3, or when you choose to share something yourself (for example a complaint, evidence, or the "Kavach protected you" image).

7. Children

Kavach is not directed at children under 13. A parent may install it on a child's phone. The Online protection and ad rules above still apply.

8. Security and retention

Data sent to the server travels over HTTPS. Reports lose weight over time and are deleted when you delete your data. Rate-limit records are deleted after two days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show a note in the app for important changes.
