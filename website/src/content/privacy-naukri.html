Ampli Naukri privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Ampli Naukri is a free app and website for government job alerts in India, made by Ampli Studio ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Android app (package com.vikash.ampli.naukri), the web app at naukri.ampliapps.in and the server behind them.

Ampli Naukri is not a government app. It is not linked to any government body or recruitment board. Every job links to its official notice, and the official notice is always the final word.

Contact for privacy questions, complaints or deletion requests: support@ampliapps.in

1. Using the app without an account

You can use Ampli Naukri without signing in.

  • Your profile stays on your phone. Your date of birth, category, state, education and marks are used on the phone to work out which jobs you can apply for. They are not sent to us.
  • Saved jobs and reminders stay on your phone. Last-date reminders are worked out on the phone.
  • The job list comes from our server. When the app fetches jobs, our server sees your IP address, like any website does. We do not link it to you (see section 5).

2. Data the app sends to us (pseudonymous)

When the app is first opened, it makes a random install ID (a random code made on your phone). It is not your name, email, phone number or a hardware ID. The app sends:

What When Why
Install ID, app version, country (from the phone's language and region settings) Once, after first run To count installs
The Play Store campaign that brought the install (only its utm_ values) Once, after first run To learn which ads and links work
Tip purchases: install ID, product (for example ampli_tip_49) and the Google Play purchase token When you give a tip To check the purchase with Google Play and stop fraud

"Share usage statistics" (off by default). In Settings, you can turn on "Share usage statistics". It works only if you are 18 or older. When it is on, the app also sends simple counts linked to the install ID:

  • how many job alerts were shown,
  • how many jobs you marked as applied,
  • your state, only as one of Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Delhi or "other" (never your profile).

You can turn it off at any time in Settings. Your install ID is shown in Settings > About, so you can quote it when you ask us to delete this data.

3. Data we keep if you sign in

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email.

  • Account: your email address, and your name and Google account ID if you use Google sign-in.
  • Profile (only after you tick the consent box): name, father's name, date of birth, category (GEN, OBC, SC, ST or EWS), gender, state, district, address, pincode, disability (PwBD) and ex-serviceman status, and education with marks. We need your category and marks because many jobs have different age limits and minimum marks for them. We record when you consented and which version of the consent text you saw.
  • Your jobs: jobs you saved or marked as applied.
  • Alerts: in-app alerts we create for you, and your choices for email and app alerts.
  • Sign-in data: we store only a scrambled (SHA-256) copy of your session token and sign-in codes, never the code or token itself.

We use this data only to match you with jobs, send the alerts you choose and keep you signed in. We do not sell or share your profile. If we ever offer coaching or other partner services, we will ask you separately each time, naming the partner and exactly what would be shared.

4. Reports you send us

If you use "Report a mistake" on a job, we keep the job, your message and the contact detail you choose to give (optional). We use it only to fix the job and, if you gave a contact, to reply to you.

5. Security and abuse limits

To stop abuse (for example, someone sending many sign-in emails), our server counts requests per IP address. These counts are kept for about an hour and then deleted. We do not use them for anything else.

6. Ads

The app may show ads from Google AdMob. AdMob can use your phone's advertising ID and other data under Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). We do not give AdMob your profile.

7. Who processes data for us

  • Amazon Web Services (servers, database and email, in the Mumbai region, India).
  • Google (Google sign-in, Google Play purchase checks and AdMob ads).

Data is encrypted while it travels (HTTPS) and the database storage is encrypted.

8. How long we keep data

Data How long
Account and profile Until you delete them, or until the account has not been used for 24 months. We email you 30 days before deleting an inactive account.
Mistake reports 12 months
Sign-in codes Deleted soon after they expire
Request counts per IP address About 1 hour
Install ID, tips and usage counts Until you ask us to delete them

9. Your rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings > Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, saved jobs and alerts from our server.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent for your profile and have it, and your saved and applied jobs, deleted from our server while keeping your account. Write to support@ampliapps.in to do this.
  • Turn off usage statistics: in the app, Settings > Share usage statistics.
  • See, correct or delete other data (including install ID data): write to support@ampliapps.in with your install ID from Settings > About. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: write to support@ampliapps.in. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Ampli Naukri is meant for job seekers. Usage statistics are sent only if you confirm you are 18 or older. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your profile, we will ask for your consent again in the app.
