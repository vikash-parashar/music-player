Oopsnaut privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Oopsnaut (“The Long Way Home”) is a platform game for Android made by Ampli Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Android app (package com.ampli.oopsnaut).

Contact for privacy questions, complaints or deletion requests: support@ampliapps.in

1. No account, no sign-in

Oopsnaut has no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Game progress and settings: levels, stars, coins, lives, characters, language, sound and control settings.
  • Player ID: a random ID (shown as “Zuno-XXXX”) made on your phone. It is not linked to your name or phone number.
  • Reminders: the “lives are full” notification is scheduled on your phone. No server sends it.

We do not upload this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video for an extra life, a full-screen ad between some levels, and a banner on the map screen. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, and may set identifiers on your phone.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choice first (Google’s User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice from the game’s Settings. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Google’s use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. App analytics (Google Firebase)

If analytics is turned on in a release, Google Analytics for Firebase records anonymous game events (for example level started, level finished, death cause, ad watched) with an app instance ID and basic device information. We use it only to balance difficulty and fix bugs. See Firebase privacy.

5. Sharing a clip

When you tap Share on a death clip, the game makes a short GIF on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

6. We don’t sell your data

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the Google services above, we share nothing with anyone.

7. Children

Oopsnaut is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has provided data, email us and we will help delete it.

8. Security and retention

Ad and analytics data are sent over encrypted connections and kept by Google under its own retention rules. Game data stays on your phone until you delete it.

9. How to delete your data

Oopsnaut has no account, so most of your data never leaves your phone.

  1. Game data (progress, coins, settings, player ID): uninstall Oopsnaut, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Oopsnaut → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes it at once.
  2. Advertising ID: go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset). Google then stops linking ad data to it.
  3. Analytics data: it is anonymous (not linked to your name, email or player ID) and Google Analytics deletes it automatically after 2 months.
  4. Anything else: email support@ampliapps.in with the subject “Oopsnaut data deletion”. We reply within 30 days and help you delete what remains.

We keep nothing else. Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules.

10. Changes

If we add online features (such as leaderboards or contests), we will update this page before they go live and change the date above.
