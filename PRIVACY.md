# Ampli MP3 Player — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Ampli MP3 Player ("Ampli", "the app") is made by Ampli Tech, India (developer: Vikash Parashar).
This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **Ampli has no accounts,
does not sell data, and your music never leaves your phone unless you choose to share or send it.**

## 1. What stays on your phone
Your songs, videos, playlists, favourites, play counts, listening stats, bookmarks, equalizer
settings, lyrics files, backups and queue are stored only on your device. Ampli does not upload
your library anywhere.

## 2. Permissions and why we ask
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Music and audio / Videos / Storage | To find and play the songs and videos on your phone, edit tags, move or delete files you choose, and save ringtones, conversions and downloads. |
| Microphone | Only when you tap **Name that song** (Ampli Pro) or voice search in Car mode. Nothing is recorded in the background. |
| Contacts | Only if you choose **One contact** when making a ringtone, to set that ringtone for the person you pick. Contacts are never uploaded. |
| Modify system settings | Only to set a ringtone, notification or alarm sound you made. |
| Notifications | To show the playback controls. |
| Internet / network state | For audiobooks, podcasts, ads, purchases, song recognition and Help & feedback (see below). |

## 3. Information that leaves your phone
- **Song recognition (Ampli Pro, optional).** When you tap Name that song, a short audio clip (about 10 seconds) is sent to our recognition provider, AudD (audd.io), to identify the song. The clip is not stored by Ampli.
- **Audiobooks and podcasts.** Searches and playback requests go directly to LibriVox / archive.org (audiobooks), Apple's public podcast directory (search and charts) and each podcast's own website (episodes). These services see your IP address and what you request, like any website.
- **Ads (free version).** The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, Ampli asks for consent first using Google's consent tool. Ampli Pro removes ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Purchases.** Ampli Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; Ampli receives only a purchase confirmation, never your card or UPI details.
- **Help & feedback (optional).** When you send a bug report, feature request or support message, Ampli sends what you type, your email only if you enter it, the app version, phone model, Android version, language, whether you have Ampli Pro, a random app-only ID (not linked to your identity), and a screenshot or app logs only if you attach them. This is used only to answer you and fix problems. Until our support server is live, these messages are sent through your own email app instead.
- **Wi-Fi transfer and Chromecast.** These work only on your local network, between your phone and your own computer or TV.

## 4. What we do not do
We do not sell or rent your data. We do not track your location. We do not read your contacts,
messages or files beyond what is described above. Ampli does not download music from unlicensed
sources.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data
Data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To delete a Help &
feedback message you sent, contact us through **Settings > Help & feedback** and mention your
reference number; we delete it within 30 days. Support messages are kept for up to 2 years.

## 6. Children
Ampli is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Security
Network requests use HTTPS. Feedback data on our server is stored with access limited to the developer.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
